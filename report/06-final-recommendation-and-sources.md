# Part 6 — Final Recommendation & Sources

## 10. Final Recommendation

**Build one local "exterior services + recurring routes" company in layers, and use tutoring as a cash floor.**

| When | Layer | Capital source | Gate to proceed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Now (Nov 2026)** | **Storefront + residential window cleaning** | ~$330 test → ~$1,250 total from starting capital | ≥5 paid recurring storefronts from 60 walk-ins |
| **Now (Nov 2026)** | **Tutoring** (personal, then agency) | ~$200 | ≥3 paying families in 21 days; waitlist by day 60 |
| **Feb–Mar 2027** | **Pet waste removal** (pre-sell in Feb, launch for the spring rush) | ~$400 test → ~$1,100 total | ≥6 paid deposits per 600 door hangers |
| **Mar–Apr 2027** | **House & concrete washing** for existing window customers | **Profits**, not starting capital (~$2,700) | ≥5 prepaid deposits before buying the machine |
| **Oct–Nov 2027** | **Holiday lights** for existing customers | Profits + 50% customer deposits | ≥5 deposits by Oct 15 |
| **Jan 2027 decision** | Campus summer storage (optional) | Customer pre-sales | ≥15 deposits by Apr 1 and campus permission |

**Why this is the right call for your situation**
- **Capital preservation:** Less than $1,000 is spent before real money comes back, and every major purchase is gated by paid demand.
- **Validated demand:** Every layer has published consumer pricing and named operators, some of whom started as students.
- **Schedule fit:** Each layer runs on early mornings, evenings, weekends, or breaks.
- **Growth beyond a side hustle:** Shared customers across 3–4 services raise revenue per customer. Route businesses can be staffed with other students and sold or handed off at graduation.
- **Honest expectations (projections):** ~$1,000/month of operating profit by month 2–3, ~$2,500–$3,500/month by month 6, and **$5,000–$10,000/month only with hired help and multiple service lines, likely 18–36 months out**.

**What would change this recommendation**
- Fewer than ~40 independent storefronts near campus → lead with **pet waste** (spring) and **tutoring** (now).
- No car → **tutoring + website/GBP** only, or walkable storefront routes.
- Either partner on an F-1 visa → check work authorization before any off-campus self-employment.
- You strongly prefer remote work and are good at sales → **website + GBP subscriptions**, using any local service customers you already have.

- **Fact (cited):** consumer price ranges, named businesses, press-reported or seller-reported revenue. Always click through and confirm.
- **Assumption (mine):** prices you'd charge, hours per job, conversion rates, cost percentages. All are listed above each table and editable in [`/model/financial_model.py`](../model/financial_model.py).
