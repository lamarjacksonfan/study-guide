# Part 1 — Executive Summary & Ranked Shortlist

*Business Opportunity Research Report for two U.S. college students · $2,000–$4,000 combined capital · 10–20 hrs/week each · Prepared November 2026*

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Bottom line:** The best risk-adjusted opportunities for you are **unglamorous, recurring, local services** that need under $1,500 of equipment, can be sold face-to-face this week, and can be worked around a class schedule. None of the top ideas are AI or e-commerce businesses. Local services won because the demand is easy to verify, a new competitor can win on reliability, and cash comes in within days.

**Top three (after the skeptical review in Part 5):**

| | Business | Why it wins | Startup cost (essential) | Base-case operating profit, month 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🥇 **Best Overall** and **Fastest Path to Cash Flow** | **Storefront + residential window cleaning** | ~$320 to test and ~$1,250 to fully equip. You walk into a shop, quote, clean, and get paid the same day. Storefront routes recur monthly, and residential jobs bring in $200–$300 each. It grows into pressure washing and holiday lights using the same customers and ladders. | ~$1,250 (~$320 to test) | ~$2,660/mo (~$1,330 per partner) |
| 🥈 **Best Long-Term Business** | **Pet waste removal subscription** | Recurring weekly revenue, the lowest skill barrier, almost no competition in many towns, and a route that can be sold. Documented operators have reached $200K–$3M+ in revenue. | ~$1,100 | ~$2,920/mo (~$1,460 per partner) |
| 🥉 **Lowest-risk benchmark** | **Student-staffed tutoring agency (local K-12 math / SAT)** | $0–$700 to start, and it uses your edge as college students. It becomes a business, not a job, once you hire other students as tutors. | ~$650 | ~$3,100/mo (~$1,550 per partner) |

**Key facts that drive the recommendation**
- **Price levels are verifiable.** Residential window cleaning averages about **$220–$310 per visit** ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/cleaning-services/clean-windows), [Fixr](https://fixr.com/costs/window-cleaning)). Weekly pet waste service runs about **$15–$30 per visit** ([Time To Pet](https://www.timetopet.com/blog/how-to-start-a-pooper-scooper-business-a-complete-guide)). SAT tutoring averages about **$62/hr** across 3,600 marketplace tutors ([Wiingy study via GlobeNewswire, Sept 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/09/30/3158961/0/en/sat-prep-tutoring-costs-63-more-than-language-learning-wiingy-study-finds.html)).
- **Real operators exist, some of them started in college.** Storage Scholars was started in a Wake Forest dorm in 2017 and reported about **$1.8M in 2022 sales** ([Shark Tank Blog](https://www.sharktankblog.com/business/storage-scholars)). Kroopin's Poopin Scoopin was **on track for about $250K of 2024 revenue, per financial documents reviewed by CNBC Make It** ([NBC/CNBC](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/how-this-37-year-old-grew-her-side-hustle-into-a-business-bringing-in-over-200000-a-year-without-a-college-degree/6063589/)). A UGA student built Detail Dawgs to **about $7,000/month** ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/college-senior-cleans-up-with-lucrative-car-detailing-side/466349)).
- **Honest ceiling.** In the base case, each top-three business reaches **$1,000/month of operating profit within about 2–3 months** and **about $3,000/month by month 6–12**. **$5,000–$10,000/month** generally needs hired help (one or two part-time workers), a second service line, and 18–36 months.
- **The benchmark to beat:** tutoring pays about **$35–$50/hr** with almost no capital. Any business you pick should beat that hourly rate within 6 months, or build an asset (a recurring route, a team) that tutoring can't.

**What to do in the next 30 days (detail in Part 5, §9):** spend **~$330** to test storefront window cleaning door-to-door before Thanksgiving. Target: **5+ paid recurring storefront commitments from 60 business visits**. In parallel, line up **3 tutoring students** at no cost. Pre-sell pet waste service for a **February–March spring launch** with a ~$230 door-hanger and deposit test. Hold the rest of your capital until a test passes.

## 2. Research Method & Screening

### 2.1 How ideas were screened
I started with ~20 candidate models and screened them against your constraints:

1. **Capital fit:** can be tested for ≤$500 and fully launched for ≤$4,000, without debt or a vehicle you don't own.
2. **Verified demand:** published consumer price data, active marketplaces, or documented operators.
3. **Schedule fit:** the work can happen evenings, weekends, early mornings, or during breaks, rather than in fixed weekday windows.
4. **Skill to start:** learnable in about 1–3 weeks with YouTube, forums, and practice.
5. **Downside:** if it fails, how much capital do you lose, and how much of it can you get back by reselling equipment?

### 2.2 Ideas screened out (and why)

| Idea | Why it didn't make the top 10 |
|---|---|
| **Trash-bin cleaning (truck/trailer model)** | The pricing is real (about $25–$37 per monthly cleaning, [Blak Sheep Creative](https://blaksheepcreative.com/trash-bin-cleaning/how-to-price-trash-bin-cleaning-route/)), but the standard setup is a truck or trailer, and guides put startup at **$25K–$120K** ([Serif](https://serif.ai/start-a-business/how-to-start-a-trash-bin-cleaning-business-2025-guide-10-steps-free-business-plan)). A manual version works only as an add-on to pressure washing (see #4). |
| **Photo booth / 360 booth rental** | Startup estimates run **$3K–$15K**, with most at around $5K ([Reservety](https://reservety.com/guides/photo-booth/how-to-start-a-photo-booth-business.html)). Revenue is weekend-event dependent and the market is crowded. It's at the top of your budget with weaker evidence. |
| **Vending machines** | Placements are competitive, one used machine eats most of your budget, and it's hard to find credible net-profit evidence at small scale. |
| **Junk removal / hauling** | Strongly proven (College Hunks Hauling Junk was founded by University of Miami students, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Hunks_Hauling_Junk)), but it needs a truck or trailer plus dump fees. Moving labor (#9) captures the student-friendly part. |
| **Bounce house / party rentals** | Insurance and liability are high, storage and transport are needed, and demand is weekend-seasonal. |
| **Dropshipping, Amazon FBA, "AI agency," social-media management** | Saturated. There's no concrete local advantage, and paid ads can burn $2K before you learn anything. Excluded per your instructions unless a validated niche existed; I didn't find one stronger than #6. |
| **Lawn care / snow removal** | Proven, but equipment-heavy (mower, trailer) and highly seasonal. Pet waste removal (#2) gives similar route economics at about 1/5th the equipment cost. |
| **General house cleaning** | Proven, but it competes with large franchises and runs in weekday daytime hours. The STR niche (#8) was kept as the higher-value version. |

### 2.3 Scoring system (used throughout)

Each idea is scored 1–10 on eight criteria (10 = best for you; for "Downside risk," **10 = safest**). The weights favor validated demand, schedule fit and capital preservation over raw upside, as you asked:

| Criterion | Weight | What a 10 means |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence of market demand | **20%** | Many documented operators plus published consumer pricing |
| Profit potential | 15% | ≥$3K/mo operating profit realistic within 12 months |
| Compatibility with college schedules | **15%** | Work can be done whenever you choose |
| Ease of execution | 12.5% | Learnable in days, few failure modes |
| Downside risk (safety) | 12.5% | Little capital at risk, low liability |
| Speed to first revenue | 10% | Paid within 1–2 weeks |
| Scalability | 10% | Can add workers or routes without you |
| Startup affordability | 5% | Well under $1,500 (low weight because all 10 fit the budget) |

**What the ranking shows**
- **Ranks 1–3 are a statistical tie (7.70–7.75).** The weights can't separate them; the qualitative critique in Part 5 does.
- **Ranks 1, 4, and 7 form one company.** Window cleaning, house washing, and holiday lights share customers, ladders, insurance, and brand, and their seasons offset each other. This "exterior services" path is the most realistic route to $5K–$10K/month (see Part 5).
- **STR cleaning has strong profit but ranks low on schedule fit.** Guest check-out to check-in windows are usually midday, which collides with classes.
- **Seasonal ideas (7, 9, 10)** produce lumpy cash. Their "month 6" figure is shown as season profit ÷ 12 so you can compare them with year-round businesses.
