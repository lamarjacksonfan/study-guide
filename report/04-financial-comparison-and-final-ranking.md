**Take-home reality check:** On ~$15,600 of pre-tax profit per partner (window base case), budget roughly **$3,000–$4,500** for self-employment tax plus income tax, depending on your state and other income. That leaves roughly **$11,000–$12,500 per person** for ~400 hours of work in year 1. That's a good student income, not a windfall.

### 4.4 What the numbers say
1. **The top five converge on ~$2,600–$3,500/month of operating profit at month 6 (base).** The difference between them is **risk, schedule fit, and evidence quality**, not profit.
2. **Effective hourly returns of ~$31–$38** in the top five beat a typical campus job, and **tutoring sets the bar** at ~$35–$50/hr with almost no capital.
3. **The website business loses on hourly return** for most of year 1 because sales time dominates.
4. **STR cleaning has the highest raw profit but blows through your time budget.** That's why it ranks 8th.
5. **Seasonal ideas** produce good season profits but small annualized income. They work best as **layers on top of a year-round business**.

**Sensitivity check (re-run with the same scores):**

| Weighting change | New #1 | Window cleaning's rank |
|---|---|---|
| Base weights | Window cleaning (7.75) | 1 |
| Equal weights on all 8 criteria | Window cleaning (8.00) | 1 |
| Downside safety ×2 | Window cleaning (7.89) | 1 |
| Scalability ×2 | Pet waste (7.75) | 2 |
| Speed ×0.5 | Pet waste (7.82) | 2 |
| Profit ×2 | Exterior washing (7.57) | 2 |
| Evidence ×2 | Pet waste (7.77) | **4** (7.63) |
| Profit ×2 **and** schedule fit ×0.5 | Exterior washing (7.60) | **4** (7.42) |

**How to read this:** The top four are within about 0.2 points under every weighting, so **the scoring can't pick a single winner**. Window cleaning wins when you prioritize **speed, low risk, and schedule fit**. Pet waste wins when you prioritize **evidence and long-term scalability**. Exterior washing wins when you prioritize **profit** and are willing to give up schedule flexibility. My recommendation in Part 5 uses this explicitly: start with window cleaning for speed and low risk, add exterior washing from profits, and build pet waste as the long-term recurring asset.
